Questions

Is remark free?

Yes. Reviewing, earning points and booking a table cost diners nothing.

When can I get the app?

remark is being tested by a first group of people in Trivandrum. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it launches.

How do you know a review is real?

A dine-in review needs a check-in within 100 metres of the restaurant. A delivery review needs a screenshot of the order and its order ID, and each order can only be used once.

Why does a restaurant have no score?

A score needs reviews from 3 different people in the last 30 days. Until then you can still read its reviews.

Where does a complaint go?

Privately to the owner, who has 48 hours to respond. If the restaurant has no owner on remark yet, it comes to us. You can follow it in the app.

Can I use remark outside Trivandrum?

You can sign up and browse from anywhere. Checking in and reviewing only work in Trivandrum for now.

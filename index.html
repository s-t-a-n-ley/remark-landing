What's different

Four questions every review platform should answer.

01

When was that review actually written?

On remark, recency isn't a filter you have to remember to apply. It's the foundation. A score is only ever the last 30 days, weighted so this week counts for more than last month.

02

Was the reviewer actually there?

Dine-in reviews need a check-in within 100 metres of the restaurant, and that distance is enforced on our servers — not in the app — so a modified client can't fake it.

03

What if you ordered in instead?

Delivery reviews need proof of a real order from the last 24 hours. Each order can be used exactly once, across every account, and it counts a little less than a visit in person.

04

Where did your last complaint go?

Here it goes straight to the owner with a clock running. They respond, or it escalates to us. You can see exactly where it stands the whole time.

Support

For diners using the remark app, and for restaurant owners

Email hello@remarks.co.in and a person on the remark team will read it.

In the app, Profile → Help & Support opens an email to the same address. Most answers are below.

Contact us

Email: hello@remarks.co.in

Write from the email address on your remark account so we can find it. If your question is about a restaurant, tell us its name and area.

Checking in

A check-in is how remark knows you were really there. If it won’t go through, it is usually one of these:

  • Distance. You need to be within 100 metres of the restaurant, with location access allowed for remark.
  • City. remark is live in Trivandrum only, for now.
  • Once a day. You can check in at the same restaurant once every 24 hours.
  • The restaurant. A delivery-only place can’t be checked into, and a restaurant that is temporarily closed or paused isn’t taking check-ins. The app tells you when this is the reason.

Writing a review

  • Dine-in reviews need a check-in at that restaurant in the last 48 hours. Each check-in backs one review.
  • Delivery reviews need the order ID from your delivery app, for an order from the last 24 hours. Each order can be reviewed once.
  • Every review needs your experience in words and at least one photo, up to four.
  • You can review the same restaurant once every 24 hours.

Editing or deleting a review

Tap the three-dot menu (⋯) on your review. You can edit it for 15 minutes after posting, and delete it at any time. Deleting a review takes back the points it earned, and does not reset the 24-hour limit for that restaurant.

Points

Points from a review unlock 24 hours after you post it, as long as the review is still up. If a review is deleted or removed, its points are taken back. Your balance is under Profile → Points & Rewards.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a review that is fake, abusive or not about the restaurant: tap the three-dot menu (⋯) on it and choose Report. Reports go to the remark team.
  • Block a reviewer from the same menu with Block, and you won’t see their reviews. To undo it, go to Profile → Blocked Accounts.

A problem with a restaurant

On the restaurant’s page, tap Report a problem with this restaurant. Use it for the food or the service, or for a listing that is wrong: the name, the location, or a place that has closed.

Complaints are private. Only you, the restaurant’s owner and the remark team can see them. Follow yours under Profile → Your Complaints.

Table bookings

Your bookings are under Profile → Your Bookings. Open one to cancel it, or to withdraw a group request the restaurant hasn’t answered yet.

Your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, at any time. Delete your account explains how, what is removed, and what to do if you can’t sign in.

What we collect and why is in the Privacy Policy, which also gives the contact for grievances. The rules for using remark are in the Terms of Service.

Restaurant owners

If you own a restaurant that is on remark, or want yours to be, email hello@remarks.co.in with the restaurant’s name and area.

What you get

Your own page

Your cover photo, hours and menu photos, with Call and Website buttons for diners.

A score out of 10

Built only from the last 30 days, so a better month shows.

Analytics

Your score over time, your ratings, dine-in and delivery reviews, and review volume.

Table bookings

Choose how many seats can be booked and how long a table is held. Large groups come to you as a request.

Complaints, privately

They come to you first, with 48 hours to respond.

Rewards

Diners can use points for ₹10, ₹30 or ₹70 off at your restaurant. You give the discount, and you can switch it off.

How reviews work

Diners prove they came

A dine-in review needs a check-in within 100 metres of your restaurant. A delivery review needs an order screenshot and order ID.

They review within 48 hours

One check-in backs one review, and each order can only be used once.

Your score updates

It uses the last 30 days only, and appears once 3 different people have reviewed you.

There is no way to pay for a better score, and we will not remove an honest review because you asked.

Questions

Can I remove a bad review?

No. We will not remove an honest review because you asked, and there is no way to pay for a better score. If you think a review breaks our rules, write to hello@remarks.co.in.

Can I reply to reviews?

Not at the moment. Complaints come to you privately, and you can respond to those.

Diners already added my restaurant. What do I do?

Start the same way. You will be taken to claim that page, and you keep its reviews and score.

Who pays for reward discounts?

Your restaurant gives the discount. You can switch rewards off at any time on the Redeem page. Codes already issued stay valid until they expire.

I run more than one outlet.

Each outlet needs its own login.

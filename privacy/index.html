Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026 · Last updated 28 July 2026

The short version. Remark verifies that reviews come from people who actually visited a restaurant. To do that we need your location at the moment you check in — and that is the most sensitive thing we collect. We never show your coordinates to restaurants or to other users.

We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. We have never sold personal data and never will. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what Remark collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have. It covers all three parts of Remark: the Remark mobile app for diners, the restaurant dashboard at restaurant.remarks.co.in, and our internal admin tools.

1Who we are

Remark is operated by Stanley Saji, a sole proprietor based in Kerala, India. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here, and you are the Data Principal.

You can reach us any time at hello@remarks.co.in.

2What we collect

Account information

Your email address, a securely hashed password, your username, display name, and optionally an avatar photo and a short bio. Restaurant owners also provide their full name and phone number.

Location — the most important section

Remark records your device's GPS coordinates at three specific moments, and only these three:

  • When you check in at a restaurant, to confirm you are within 100 metres of it.
  • When you submit a dine-in review, carried over from that check-in.
  • When you like a review, because likes require you to be at the restaurant.

We collect location only while the app is open and in the foreground. Remark does not use background location, does not track your movements between visits, and does not build a location history beyond those individual verification events. We do not request the "Always allow" location permission.

Your coordinates are private. They are never shown to restaurants, never shown to other users, and never displayed anywhere in the app. A restaurant only learns that a verified visit happened — never where you were standing, and never anything about where you go otherwise.

Reviews and photos

Your star rating, review text, any dishes you tag, and up to five photos per review. Reviews and their photos are public — that is the point of the product.

Delivery order proof

If you review a delivery order, we collect the platform (Swiggy, Zomato, or other), the order ID, the order date, optionally the order amount, and a screenshot of your order confirmation. We use these solely to verify the order was real and to prevent the same order being reviewed twice.

Please crop your screenshots. Order confirmations often contain your home address and phone number. Only upload the portion showing the restaurant, order ID, and date. Uploaded images are stored at web addresses that are not listed or searchable but are not individually access-controlled, so treat anything you upload as potentially viewable by someone with the link.

We also do not currently strip camera metadata (EXIF) from uploaded images, which can include the location where a photo was taken. If that concerns you, disable location tagging in your camera app.

Restaurant owner information

For owners: your business details, your FSSAI licence number and a copy of the licence document, and your restaurant's address and location. FSSAI documents are stored privately and are visible only to you and to our review team.

Activity data

Your points balance and points history, reward redemptions, complaints you raise, and reports you file about other reviews.

Technical data

On the web dashboards, we set strictly necessary session cookies to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies. On mobile, your session is stored securely on your device (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and no cookies are used.

3What we deliberately do not collect

We think this matters as much as what we do collect. Remark contains:

  • No analytics software. No Google Analytics, PostHog, Mixpanel, Firebase, or equivalent.
  • No advertising networks or third-party trackers of any kind.
  • No crash-reporting or session-recording tools.
  • No device fingerprinting — we do not collect advertising IDs, device identifiers, or hardware information.
  • No IP address logging in the application, and no browsing history.
  • No background location and no movement tracking.
  • No payment card data. We do not process card payments at all.
  • No contact list, microphone, calendar, or camera-roll scanning. We only receive the specific photos you choose to upload.

4Why we use your data

  • Verification — confirming a review comes from a real visit. This is the core of Remark.
  • Scoring — calculating each restaurant's rolling 30-day score.
  • Fraud prevention — detecting fake reviews, duplicate orders, and coordinated review attacks.
  • Rewards — awarding points and issuing redemption codes.
  • Communication — sending transactional email such as confirmation codes, reward codes, and complaint updates. We do not send marketing email.
  • Support and safety — resolving complaints and enforcing our Terms.

We process this data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account and when you grant location permission, as required by the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

6Who we share data with

We share personal data with exactly three service providers, each of which processes it only to run Remark:

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file storage provider. All account data, reviews, and uploads are stored on Supabase infrastructure.
  • Google Maps Platform — provides maps and place search. Map usage sends the map view and search queries to Google. Your stored check-in coordinates are not sent to Google.
  • Resend — delivers our transactional emails. Resend receives your email address and the contents of that email.

We may also disclose data where legally required, for example in response to a valid order from an Indian court or law-enforcement authority.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. There is no third-party advertising on Remark.

Some of these providers process and store data on servers outside India. The DPDP Act permits such transfers except to countries specifically restricted by the Government of India.

7How long we keep data, and how to delete it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the mobile app. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes:

  • your profile, username, avatar and bio;
  • every review you have written, and every photo attached to them;
  • all your check-ins and all stored GPS coordinates;
  • your points balance, points history, and any unused reward codes;
  • your delivery order proofs and screenshots;
  • your complaints, reports and likes;
  • your login credentials.

There is no recovery period — once deleted, your data cannot be restored, and any unused points are forfeited. Affected restaurant scores are recalculated so your removed reviews no longer count.

Two things survive deletion. Internal records of moderation actions (for example, if a review was removed for abuse) are retained so we can enforce our rules consistently. Restaurant scores are aggregate numbers that no longer reference you in any way.

If your account is linked to a restaurant listing, email us instead so we can transfer the listing before deleting your account.

8Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data — most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Erase your data, via in-app deletion or by writing to us.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Complain to us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To exercise any of these, email hello@remarks.co.in. We respond within 30 days.

10How we protect your data

  • Row-level security on every database table — access rules are enforced by the database itself, not just by app code, so they cannot be bypassed by a modified client.
  • Your coordinates are not readable by the public API, only by our verification logic on the server.
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) for all connections, and encryption at rest for stored data.
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form — we cannot see your password.
  • Sessions are stored in secure device storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Verification rules run server-side, so proximity checks and review limits cannot be forged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.
